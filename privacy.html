Privacy

What I collect, why, and what I will never do with it.

This site belongs to me, Liru Wang, an executive coach based in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. I collect as little as I can, and only what I need to reply to you or send you something you asked for. If you have a question about anything here, write to me at liru@leadershiptransitionintelligence.com.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What I collect, and why

The early-reader list

When you join, I collect your first name and email address so I can send you Chapter One of Lead Through It, news when the book is out, and occasional notes on leading through transition. You will receive a confirmation email first, and nothing more is sent unless you click to confirm. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and one click takes you off the list.

The LTQ Self-Assessment

When you submit the assessment, your answers, your scores, your result, your first name (if you give it), and your email address come to me. I use them to understand your result if you choose to talk with me, and to reply if you write back. Submitting the assessment does not add you to any mailing list.

The contact form and booking a conversation

When you send a message, I receive your name, email address, and what you wrote. When you book a discovery conversation, the scheduling service collects the details you enter there so we can meet. I use these only to reply and to hold our conversation.

What I will never do

  • Sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • Add you to a mailing list without your clear consent.
  • Share your assessment answers or your messages with anyone else.

Services that help run this site

A few trusted services handle information on my behalf. Some of them store data outside Canada, including in the United States.

  • Kit manages the early-reader list and sends those emails.
  • Formspree delivers contact form messages and assessment submissions to me.
  • Calendly handles booking on the Connect page and may set its own cookies there.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, may keep basic technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google.
  • Gmail is where emails sent to me through this site arrive.

This site does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

How long I keep it

You stay on the early-reader list until you unsubscribe. Messages and assessment submissions are kept only as long as I need them to reply to you and to keep a reasonable record of our contact, and I delete them sooner if you ask.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email liru@leadershiptransitionintelligence.com and I will take care of it, usually within a few days.

If you become a coaching client

What we discuss in coaching is confidential. It is covered by our coaching agreement and the International Coaching Federation Code of Ethics, not by this website notice.

Changes

If I change how I handle your information, I will update this page and the date at the top.
