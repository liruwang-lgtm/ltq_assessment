The Leadership Transition Check-In

12 statements · about 3 minutes

Where are you right now in your leadership?

Every leader moves through seasons: times when everything feels new, times when things run smoothly but something feels missing, and times when bigger questions start to surface. Each season asks something different of you.

This check-in helps you see which season you're in right now. It shows where you are now, not what you are capable of, and there are no right or wrong answers.

Choose how true each statement has been for you over the past two to three months. Go with your first honest answer.

1 = Not at all true · 2 = Rarely · 3 = Sometimes · 4 = Often · 5 = Very true
