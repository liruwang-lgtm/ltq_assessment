For leaders

One-to-one executive coaching

This is for you if

  • You have stepped into a role that is bigger than the one you know how to do, and the gap is not closing as fast as you expected
  • You are competent and successful, and something quietly important feels missing
  • You are asking questions about direction and meaning that your current role has no place to put
  • You are leading across cultures and carrying a complexity that standard leadership advice does not name

What changes

You finish this work able to name what is happening to you, having released the version of yourself the last role rewarded, and carrying a method you can use again. The last part matters most. Each new role begins a new Taking-Off, at a higher altitude, with more of yourself to bring to it.

How it works

We begin with the LTQ Self-Assessment and a conversation about what it surfaced. From there the work is shaped around your stage and the layer most in need of attention: identity, relationships, or your standing in the wider system.

The role changes on announcement day. The identity changes through deliberate, sustained inner work.

Engagements run six to twelve months, because identity shifts more slowly than skills. Session rhythm is set with you rather than fixed in advance, because what a leader needs in the first month of a new role is not what they need in the eighth.

For organizations

When a promotion is the start of the risk, not the end of it

Too many newly promoted leaders stall, or leave, in their first year. They were chosen for what they could do, and the new role asks them to become someone different. For HR and learning and development leaders, I bring the same work into your organization so that a transition is supported rather than survived: a cohort brought up to speed together, a team given shared language, or a leadership audience given a new way to see what is happening to them.

Each program is built into what you already run, with the LTQ Self-Assessment and the same framework and vocabulary as Lead Through It.

Group executive coaching

A cohort of up to fifteen leaders moving through transitions at the same time. The cap is deliberate: this is coaching, not teaching, and everyone needs to be heard.

Where it fits

  • Newly promoted leaders who would otherwise each work it out alone, slowly
  • A high-potential group being prepared for roles not yet given
  • A leadership population in the same restructure, merger, or new mandate

What the group adds is recognition: the disorientation each leader assumed was personal turns out to be structural.

Workshops

The LTQ framework applied with a team or a leadership population, in your context, with your language and your constraints.

Where it fits

  • A leadership development program that needs a transition module with substance
  • A team in the middle of a restructure or a new mandate
  • An organization building transition capability rather than handling it case by case

Participants leave with a shared vocabulary for something they had no words for. Teams keep using it long after the session ends.

Practical questions

Where do you work?
With leaders and organizations worldwide. Coaching is virtual; workshops and keynotes can be virtual or in person.
How long are workshops and keynotes?
Keynotes typically run 45 to 60 minutes. Workshops range from a half day to a full day, or a series of shorter sessions built into your program.
What happens in the first conversation?
Thirty minutes, at no cost. We talk about what is changing for you or your leaders and whether this work is the right fit. If you have taken the assessment, bring the result.
How does pricing work?
Engagements are scoped to your situation. We will discuss investment in our first conversation.

Where to start

If you are the leader: start with the assessment. Six minutes, eighteen statements, and you will know which stage you are in and what it is asking of you. Bring the result to a conversation and we will have somewhere concrete to begin.
